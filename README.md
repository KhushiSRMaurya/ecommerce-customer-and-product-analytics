# E-Commerce Customer & Product Analytics

## About the Project

This project looks at e-commerce sales, customer behaviour, product performance, and returns using Power BI.

The report has two pages. The first focuses on overall sales and profit performance, while the second looks at customer segments, returning customers, and product returns.

## Dashboard Overview

### 1. Sales Performance
- Total revenue, profit, orders, and customers
- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Revenue and profit by category
- Top products by revenue
- Regional sales performance
- Sales channel contribution

### 2. Customer and Product Analysis
- Customer and return order counts
- Return rate
- New and returning customers
- Customer segments
- Top customers
- Product profitability
- Revenue and profit by category and subcategory

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Data visualisation

## What I Learned

This project helped me practise building interactive reports, working with business KPIs, and presenting sales and customer data in a way that is easier to understand.
